Privacy policy

How PAWS uses information from people who contact, volunteer with, or donate to the organization.

Contact and applications

PAWS uses the details you submit through Google Forms, such as your name, contact information, interests, and availability, to respond to inquiries and coordinate participation.

Donations and the calendar

Stripe handles payment details and checkout. The Events page displays a Google Calendar. Those services may use cookies when you interact with them.

Your information

PAWS does not sell your personal information. To request access, a correction, or deletion, email pawsforacausemi@gmail.com.
